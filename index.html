About Our College

OUR HISTORY

Bangladesh Air Force Shaheen College Chattogram, inspired by the spirit of the Shaheen aphorism, developed on a foundation of academic excellence and discipline. The institution is situated near the estuary of the Karnaphuli River and the Bay of Bengal, within the serene and secure premises of Bangladesh Air Force Base Zahurul Haque, Patenga.

The expansive campus features lush green playgrounds, well-maintained facilities, and a tranquil environment, providing an ideal setting for learning and holistic development.

Established in 1978 as a Bengali Medium school and named in honour of Shaheed Sergeant Zahurul Haque, the institution gradually expanded to Higher Secondary level in 1985–86. The English Version of the school section was introduced in 2006, while the college section expanded to Higher Secondary English Version in 2014.
